The Ritual — 09

Four touches, zero spray

1

Slide

Thumb the heavy brass lid open. One fluid motion with a single hand — in a cab or boarding lounge.

2

Swirl

Gently warm fingertip on the balm for 2–3 seconds. Your body heat softens just the right dose of botanical wax.

3

Trace

Press balm to pulse points: wrists, collarbones, behind ears. Never rub harshly; allow the lipids to melt quietly into skin.

4

Re-touch

Reapply whenever you desire without clouding the room. One 12g compact gives ~500 personal touches.

Our Origins — 10

Born Between
Bombay & Kannauj

After years working with traditional perfume houses in Grasse and London, we watched Indian fragrance shelves fill with European alcohol sprays that vaporized within two hours of Mumbai or Delhi humidity.

VĀRA was founded to restore India’s oil and wax heritage into modern, spill-proof brass objects. We blend slow botanical distillates with kokum butter from Maharashtra and certified plantation Mysore sandalwood — creating personal scents that live on your skin.
