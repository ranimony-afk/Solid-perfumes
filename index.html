Form & Function — 03

Why solid outwears spray

Sprays are 85% alcohol that flashes off in tropical heat. Solids are perfume held in skin-melting wax — intimate, exact, enduring.

01
◍

Skin-melting, not evaporating

Kokum & beeswax bind to skin lipids, releasing absolutes steadily for 6+ hours — no alcohol blast, no dry-down shock.

02
✈

Pocket, purse & flight proof

Solid brass sliders ignore cabin pressure, gym bags and security trays. Zero leaks. Zero glass. Zero apologies.

03
☾

Discreet, close sillage

Trace wrists, collarbone, behind ears. Only those you embrace will know. Polite luxury for lifts, cabins & meetings.

04
❧

Less waste, more ritual

One 12g compact ≈ 4–6 months. Refill forever. No propellant, no atomiser plastic, no half-evaporated bottle.

The Ritual — 09

Four touches, zero spray

1

Slide

Thumb the brass lid open. One hand, one motion — even in a cab or cabin.

2

Swirl

Circle a fingertip 2–3 seconds. Body heat liquefies just enough wax.

3

Trace

Press to wrists, neck hollow, behind ears. Never rub — let wax settle.

4

Re-touch

Reapply invisibly every 4–5 hours. One compact = ~500 applications.
